WEB DESIGN AGREEMENT

Client: Bottega Rossi S.r.l.
Freelancer: Marco Bianchi, web developer
Date: 1 October 2026
Fee: EUR 2,400 (fixed price)

1. DELIVERABLES
The Freelancer will design and build a company showcase website consisting of five (5) pages: Home, About Us, Products, Gallery, Contact.
The website will be responsive and built on WordPress with a premium theme chosen by the Client.
The Contact page will include one contact form that sends submissions to the Client's email address.
The Freelancer will perform basic on-page SEO (titles, meta descriptions) and image optimisation for all five pages.

2. CONTENT
All texts and photos will be provided by the Client in Italian before 10 October 2026.
The Freelancer will insert the provided content into the pages. Copywriting is not included.

3. REVISIONS
The fee includes two (2) rounds of revisions on the design and layout. Each round may include multiple changes submitted together.
Additional revision rounds will be billed at EUR 50 per hour.

4. EXCLUSIONS
The following are not included in this agreement: e-commerce or online payments, user accounts or login areas, multilingual versions, copywriting, hosting fees, and ongoing maintenance.

5. SUPPORT
After launch, the Freelancer will fix bugs and errors in the delivered work free of charge for thirty (30) days.

6. TIMELINE
Launch is planned for 31 October 2026.
