IT SUPPORT RETAINER AGREEMENT

Client: Studio Dentistico Ferri (Bari)
Consultant: Andrea Greco, freelance IT consultant
Start date: 1 September 2026
Fee: EUR 400 per month

1. SERVICES INCLUDED
Up to six (6) hours per month of remote support for the Client's 8 workstations and 1 file server.
Creating, modifying and removing Microsoft 365 user accounts for the Client's staff.
Monitoring of the nightly backups and restoring files when needed.
Installation of Windows updates and management of the antivirus on the 8 workstations and the server.
One (1) on-site visit per month, on a date agreed with the Client.

2. RESPONSE TIME
Requests sent by email are handled by the next business day, Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00.

3. NOT INCLUDED
Purchase of hardware or software licences.
Support for the dental practice management software, which is provided by its vendor.
Support for staff personal devices.
Emergency interventions outside business hours, billed at EUR 80 per hour.
Projects such as migrations, new installations or network redesigns, which will be quoted separately.

4. HOURS
Hours beyond the monthly allowance are billed at EUR 55 per hour. Unused hours do not carry over to the following month.
